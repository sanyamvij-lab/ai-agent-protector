Permission & Privacy Explainer (§7.6)

Least-Privilege Principles: AI Protector is architected with strict data minimization:

  • webNavigation: Used exclusively to evaluate destination URLs before page rendering. Your full URLs are normalized and credentials are completely stripped prior to threat queries.
  • storage: Retains your trusted domains and temporary 15-minute informed overrides in your browser's private local storage.
  • contextMenus: Enables on-demand right-click threat analysis on any selected text or hyperlink without ambient monitoring.
  • Zero Tracking: Visited URLs, web page DOM contents, and browsing history are never logged or recorded.
