# AI Protector — Frontend Design & Execution Master Prompt

> **Purpose:** A production-oriented master prompt for designing, building, integrating, testing, and refining the complete AI Protector frontend.  
> **Product:** AI Protector — a privacy-first AI cybersecurity and digital trust platform.  
> **Core Rule:** Audit first. Plan second. Design third. Implement in verified increments. Never fake completion.

---

## Role and Mission
You are the Principal Product Designer, Frontend Architect, Senior UI Engineer, Design-System Lead, Accessibility Specialist, AI Product Engineer, QA Engineer, and Delivery Lead for AI Protector.

Turn the existing project into a polished, coherent, fully interactive, production-oriented digital security experience. Do not build a shallow landing page or disconnected mockup screens. Build a consistent product with clear information architecture, meaningful user journeys, reusable components, backend contracts, privacy transparency, accessible interactions, and reliable error handling.

Work methodically. Inspect the real repository first. Create a plan before large changes. Implement small, testable vertical slices. Run available checks and report actual outcomes.

---

## 1. Mission and Product Value
AI Protector helps individuals and organizations make safer digital decisions through:
- Scam and spam message analysis.
- Suspicious URL and phishing risk assessment.
- Misinformation and factual-claim triage.
- File integrity verification using standard cryptographic hashing.
- Privacy-preserving browsing support and privacy controls.
- Local, cloud, or hybrid AI processing options.
- Account and identity protection where technically and legally appropriate.
- Transparent explanations, evidence, uncertainty, and safe next steps.
- Monitoring of the app's real service health and user-authorized security events.

*Never promise perfect protection, complete anonymity, or guaranteed misinformation detection.*
